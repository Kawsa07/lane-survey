How a survey works

Four steps, in this order, from your first call to the final map.

  1. Tell us about the plot

    Share the location and any deeds or maps you have.

  2. Site visit

    Our team measures the plot with total station and GPS equipment.

  3. Mark the corners

    We set stakes or concrete pillars at each boundary point.

  4. Receive your map

    You get a signed plan with bearings, distances and area.

Checked twice

Every measurement is repeated from a second reference point before we mark it.

Fixed, written price

You get the full cost in writing before work starts.

Plans you can use

Clear maps accepted for registration, loans, building permits and court files.
